# Build an evidence brief

Your task is to advise a coastal research partnership with a limited fieldwork budget. The partnership wants better visibility of fishing pressure and better understanding of how marine ecosystems support population health. It can fund one follow-up study, not a universal ocean dashboard.

## Deliver five connected artifacts

1. **An observation map.** Select a region in notebook 01. Include a clear unit, period, scale, projection, attribution, and a statement about missing areas. Explain what the study product measures.
2. **A sensitivity analysis.** Vary a matching gate, radar threshold, or coverage requirement. Report the changes in both numerator and denominator. Do not choose a threshold solely because it creates a dramatic story.
3. **A One Health pathway.** Choose nutrition, livelihoods, environmental exposure, or ecosystem resilience. Identify an outcome, two mechanisms, and at least three possible confounders. Separate measurements from assumptions.
4. **A realistic data plan.** State which data already exist, which require permission, which require new sampling, and which would be too large for JupyterLite. Give each dataset a citation, version, license, and data dictionary.
5. **A decision memo.** Recommend one bounded next step. Explain who should participate in the decision, what could falsify your interpretation, and which uncertainty would most change your recommendation.

## Questions that strengthen the brief

Could unequal AIS reception produce the apparent difference? Is the comparison between cells of equal area? Are detections being mistaken for unique vessels? Do the satellite, fishery, environmental, and health datasets refer to the same time period and population? Does the health outcome describe individuals or aggregates? Could trade or price changes alter nutrition without changing fish abundance?

## Assessment rubric

| Criterion | Strong evidence brief | Common failure |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | Describes the measurement and its support | Calls model output ground truth |
| Reproducibility | Re-runnable notebook, parameters, versions and sources | A screenshot without provenance |
| Uncertainty | Competing explanations and a sensitivity plot | A single percentage without denominator |
| Health reasoning | Measurable outcome and explicit causal pathway | Infers disease from a vessel hotspot |
| Equity | Includes affected communities and distribution | Uses global totals as local welfare |
| Decision | Bounded action tied to evidence | Unsupported enforcement accusation |
