## Additional data you may bring

Current Global Fishing Watch platform/API exports are subject to their own [terms of use](https://globalfishingwatch.org/terms-of-use/), currently described as non-commercial use under CC BY-NC 4.0. This differs from the CC BY 4.0 license on the **specific publication-data deposit** bundled here. Do not apply the deposit's license to a newly downloaded platform export. Complete GFW's requested citation with the dataset version, time range, retrieval date and access route. Historical archives may carry different terms; follow their version-specific metadata.

Large optical, VIIRS, ocean-color, bathymetry, population, health and fisheries-assessment datasets are suggested follow-up sources, not undisclosed inputs to the simulations. The only observed SST input is the bounded NOAA subset above; it is displayed separately and does not generate the synthetic series. Record exact products, licenses, resolution, uncertainty and transformations if you add more. Restricted patient or identifiable worker data should not be committed to this public teaching repository.
